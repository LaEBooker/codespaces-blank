1: The game is a run and gun, somewhat like subway surfers and is heavily inspired by Jetpack Joyride. The player is 
trying to aviod obsticals and enemies to get the highest score they can get.

2: The game has a short period for you to know that the round has started, from there the game is constantly moving foward.

3: The game objects would be the player, the enemy, the obstical, the power-ups, and the elements that help you stay up in the air.

4: The controls are very simple. The only one button is required to avoid obsticals and move foward. Holding it makes you go
higher and you're trying not to hit anything that may be on the ceiling or ground of the enviornment.

5: The ground enemy would walk around spawning on different sides of the screen to stop you from landing on the ground. When you get 
touched by them the round is over, or when you have a power-up you lose said power-up. The spikes on the ceiling would stop you from staying
too high from the ground. So you would have to stay somewhere in the middle.

6: The lose condition is hitting any of the enemies or obsticals in your way, then you restart and do it again.
